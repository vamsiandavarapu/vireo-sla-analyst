### Key Operational Finding: Overnight Queue Accumulation

A surface-level review of roster metrics suggests that the **Tier 1 Morning Shift (06:00 – 14:00 IST)** suffers from the worst performance, with a breach rate of **32.16%** (1,866 breaches). However, detailed operational decomposition reveals a crucial operational insight:

1. **Overnight Origin:** **77.81%** (1,452 out of 1,866) of all Morning shift breaches were generated by tickets submitted overnight during off-peak hours **(22:00 – 06:00 IST)**.
2. **In-Shift vs. Overnight Comparison:**
   - Morning tickets created during normal daytime hours (06:00 – 22:00 IST) maintain a healthy breach rate of **11.02%**.
   - Morning tickets created overnight (22:00 – 06:00 IST) experience a staggering breach rate of **70.93%**.
3. **Operational Root Cause:** Morning frontline agents are not underperforming during their active shift. Instead, they inherit a accumulated backlog of unassigned overnight tickets at 06:00 AM IST that instantly breach their SLA timers upon shift startup.
